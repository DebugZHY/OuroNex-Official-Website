Why structural engineering

We start where precision is not optional.

Structural work is governed by codes, checked by peers and signed by professionals. That is exactly the environment where domain-grounded AI proves its worth — and where generic AI proves its limits.

  1. Step 01

    AI prepares

    Drafts, checks and documentation are assembled with domain knowledge and your project context.

  2. Step 02

    Evidence attached

    Outputs are prepared with references and reasoning attached, so a reviewer can see how each result was reached.

  3. Checkpoint

    Professional review

    A qualified engineer examines the work against codes, standards and engineering judgement.

  4. Step 04

    The engineer decides

    Approval, revision or rejection — the final call, and the signature, belong to the professional.

AI assists. It never signs.
